Force

Understand force, Newton's laws, friction, momentum and the effect of force on moving objects.

1. What is Force?

Force is a push or pull that can change the state of motion, direction or shape of an object.

Force is a vector quantity because it has both magnitude and direction.

SI Unit of Force = Newton (N)

2. Effects of Force

  • It can make a stationary object move.
  • It can stop a moving object.
  • It can increase or decrease the speed.
  • It can change the direction of motion.
  • It can change the shape of an object.

3. Newton's First Law

An object remains at rest or continues moving with uniform velocity in a straight line unless an external unbalanced force acts on it.

This law is also called the Law of Inertia.

Example

When a bus suddenly stops, passengers tend to move forward because their bodies tend to remain in their state of motion.

4. Newton's Second Law

Newton's second law states that the acceleration produced in an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass.

F = ma

Where:
F = Force
m = Mass
a = Acceleration

5. Newton's Third Law

For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Examples

  • Walking
  • Swimming
  • Rocket propulsion
  • Jumping from the ground

6. Balanced and Unbalanced Forces

Balanced forces are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction. Their net force is zero.

Net Force = 0

Unbalanced forces produce a non-zero net force and can change the motion of an object.

Net Force ≠ 0

7. Friction

Friction is a force that opposes the relative motion between two surfaces in contact.

  • Static friction
  • Sliding friction
  • Rolling friction
  • Fluid friction

Friction can be useful, such as during walking and braking, but it can also cause energy loss.

8. Weight and Mass

Mass is the amount of matter present in an object. Weight is the gravitational force acting on that object.

Weight = mg

m = mass and g = acceleration due to gravity.

9. Numerical Example

Question

A force of 30 N acts on an object of mass 5 kg. Find its acceleration.

Given:

F = 30 N
m = 5 kg

Formula:

F = ma

Rearranging:

a = F ÷ m

Calculation:

a = 30 ÷ 5 = 6 m/s²

Answer: Acceleration = 6 m/s²

10. Quick Revision

  • Force is a push or pull.
  • Force is a vector quantity.
  • SI unit of force is Newton (N).
  • Newton's first law is the law of inertia.
  • Newton's second law: F = ma.
  • Newton's third law: action and reaction.
  • Friction opposes relative motion.
  • Weight = mg.
