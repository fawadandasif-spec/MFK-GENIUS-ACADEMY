⚡ Electric Charge

Federal Board Physics — Electricity Chapter

1. What is Electric Charge?

Electric charge is a physical property of matter that causes objects to experience electric forces.

Electric charge is represented by the symbol Q or q and its SI unit is the coulomb (C).

SI Unit of Charge = Coulomb (C)

2. Types of Electric Charge

Type Example Behavior
Positive Charge Proton Repels positive, attracts negative
Negative Charge Electron Repels negative, attracts positive
Remember: Like charges repel each other, while unlike charges attract each other.

3. Important Properties of Electric Charge

  • There are two types of charge: positive and negative.
  • Like charges repel each other.
  • Unlike charges attract each other.
  • Electric charge is conserved.
  • Electric charge is quantized.
  • Charge is a scalar quantity.

4. Conservation of Charge

The law of conservation of charge states that electric charge can neither be created nor destroyed. It can only be transferred from one object to another.

Example:
When two objects are rubbed together, electrons may transfer from one object to the other. The total charge of the system remains conserved.

5. Quantization of Charge

Electric charge exists in discrete amounts. The charge on an object is an integral multiple of the elementary charge.

Q = ne

Where:

  • Q = total charge
  • n = number of electrons or elementary charges
  • e = elementary charge = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C

6. Coulomb's Law

Coulomb's law states that the electrostatic force between two point charges is directly proportional to the product of their charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

F = kq₁q₂ / r²

Where:

  • F = electrostatic force
  • q₁ and q₂ = two charges
  • r = distance between the charges
  • k = Coulomb's constant
k = 9 × 10⁹ N m²/C²

7. Solved Numerical

Question:
Two charges of 2 C and 3 C are separated by a distance of 2 m. Find the electrostatic force between them.

Given:

  • q₁ = 2 C
  • q₂ = 3 C
  • r = 2 m
  • k = 9 × 10⁹ N m²/C²

Formula:

F = kq₁q₂ / r²

Solution:

F = (9 × 10⁹ × 2 × 3) / (2²)

F = 13.5 × 10⁹ N

⭐ Important Points
