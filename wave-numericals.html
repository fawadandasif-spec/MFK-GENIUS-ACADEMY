Wave Numericals

Practice important wave and sound numericals using wavelength, frequency, time period, wave speed, amplitude and electromagnetic wave formulas.

📚 Important Wave Formulas

v = fλ
f = 1/T
T = 1/f
λ = v/f
f = v/λ

Numerical 1 — Find Wave Speed

A wave has a frequency of 5 Hz and wavelength of 4 m. Find its speed.

v = fλ

v = 5 × 4

Answer: v = 20 m/s

Numerical 2 — Find Wavelength

A wave travels with a speed of 30 m/s and has a frequency of 6 Hz. Find its wavelength.

λ = v/f

λ = 30 ÷ 6

Answer: λ = 5 m
