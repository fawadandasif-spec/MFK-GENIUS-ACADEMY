10 • Common Mistakes & Internal Choice

TopicTrapMFK Fix
IndicesWrong law for addition/subtractionWrite the law first.
LogsDropping base/signKeep base visible.
SetsWrong Venn regionShade systematically.
FactorizationExpanding too earlyIdentify pattern first.
InequalityNo sign reversalCircle negative multiply/divide step.
TrigWrong sideLabel triangle.
Coordinatesx/y swapCopy ordered pair carefully.
StatisticsBoundary/mark confusionCheck exact formula required.
ProbabilityAnswer outside 0–1Recount favourable/total.

Internal choice: If both options are familiar, choose the one with the cleaner diagram/formula and fewer risky transformations — but only after reading the full question.

Q5. Solve a linear equation with fractional coefficients.
Q6. Solve a right-triangle trigonometric problem.
Q7. Find midpoint and gradient of two points.
Q8. Find a line equation from point + gradient.
Q9. Find a regular-polygon angle.
Q10. Describe/perform a required triangle construction.
Q11. Find grouped-data mean or median.
Q12. Find probability from ungrouped outcomes.

Section C — Long-Answer Training

Q3. Sets: solve a three-set Venn problem and prove the relevant De Morgan identity.
Q4. Algebra: solve a square-root/HCF/LCM style expression with complete working.
Q5. Trigonometry & Bearing: solve a complete bearing/elevation problem with a labelled diagram.
Q6. Coordinate/Straight Lines: derive a line equation and verify the gradient relationship.
Q7. Practical Geometry: construct the required triangle and bisectors and identify concurrency.
Q8. Statistics: calculate grouped mean/median/mode as required and check the result.
