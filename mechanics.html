1. What is Mechanics?

Mechanics is the branch of physics that studies the motion of objects and the forces that cause or change that motion.

It helps us understand how objects move, why objects accelerate, how forces act and how energy and momentum are transferred.

2. Main Areas of Mechanics

  • Kinematics: Describes motion without discussing its causes.
  • Dynamics: Studies motion and the forces causing it.
  • Statics: Studies objects in equilibrium.

3. Motion

Motion occurs when an object's position changes with respect to a reference point and time.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time
Velocity = Displacement ÷ Time
Acceleration = Change in Velocity ÷ Time

Example

A car travels 100 metres in 20 seconds. Its average speed is:

Speed = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s

4. Force

Force is a push or pull that can change the motion, direction or shape of an object.

F = ma

The SI unit of force is Newton (N).

5. Newton's Laws of Motion

First Law

An object remains at rest or continues moving with uniform velocity unless an external unbalanced force acts on it.

Second Law

F = ma

Third Law

For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.

6. Work

Work is done when a force causes an object to move through a distance in the direction of the force.

W = Fd

Where W is work, F is force and d is displacement. The SI unit of work is Joule (J).

7. Energy

Energy is the ability to do work.

Kinetic Energy

Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its motion.

KE = ½mv²

Potential Energy

Potential energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its position or configuration.

PE = mgh

8. Power

Power is the rate at which work is done.

Power = Work ÷ Time

The SI unit of power is Watt (W).

9. Momentum

Momentum is the product of the mass and velocity of an object.

p = mv

Momentum is a vector quantity and its SI unit is kg m/s.

10. Conservation of Momentum

In an isolated system, the total momentum before an interaction is equal to the total momentum after the interaction.

Total Momentum Before = Total Momentum After

11. Solved Numerical

Question

An object of mass 10 kg is moving with an acceleration of 3 m/s². Find the force acting on the object.

Given:

m = 10 kg
a = 3 m/s²

Formula:

F = ma

Calculation:

F = 10 × 3 = 30 N

Answer: Force = 30 N

12. Quick Revision

  • Mechanics deals with motion and forces.
  • Kinematics describes motion.
  • Dynamics studies forces and motion.
  • Force = ma.
  • Work = Force × displacement.
  • Kinetic Energy = ½mv².
  • Potential Energy = mgh.
  • Power = Work ÷ Time.
  • Momentum = mv.
  • Momentum is conserved in an isolated system.
